ABOUT US

Growing Young Minds
With Strong Values

Olive Tree Global Education Campus was established with a clear educational philosophy: children achieve their true potential when learning is inspiring, exploratory and meaningful. Our aim is to create an environment in which students enjoy coming to school, participate actively in learning and progressively develop the knowledge, values and life skills required for the future. We recognise that every child possesses unique abilities. Our responsibility as educators is therefore not simply to deliver lessons, but to identify, encourage and nurture those abilities. Through academics, technology, sports, co-curricular programmes, cultural exposure and personality development, Olive Tree strives to provide a balanced educational experience. The ultimate purpose of an Olive Tree education is to help children become confident, knowledgeable, compassionate and responsible citizens who make their families, school and society proud.
