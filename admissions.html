HOW IT WORKS

Admission Procedure

Our admission process is designed to be clear, supportive and convenient for families.

01
✉

Admission Enquiry

Parents may contact the school or submit the online Admission Enquiry Form.

02
🏫

School Interaction / Campus Visit

Parents may be invited to visit the campus to understand the school's educational philosophy, academic programme, facilities and student-development opportunities.

03
📝

Application Form

The prescribed admission application form must be completed with accurate student and parent/guardian information.

04
📂

Submission of Documents

Parents should submit the documents requested by the school along with the completed application.

05
⭐

Student Interaction / Assessment

Where applicable, the school may conduct an age-appropriate student interaction or academic assessment.

06
✓

Admission Confirmation

Admission will be confirmed subject to eligibility, availability of seats, submission and verification of required documents, and compliance with applicable school/CBSE admission requirements.

07
₹

Fee Payment & Enrolment

After confirmation, parents may complete the prescribed fee formalities to secure the admission.
