INTERNATIONAL RECOGNITION

Excellence in Education 2026

Honoured at the UK Parliament

Olive Tree was honoured at the UK Parliament with the Excellence in Education 2026 Award. Our Chairman, Dr. Isaac Livingstone, received this recognition from Lord Daniel Brennan KC, an Advisory Committee member to King Charles III, as described by the school. Being recognised at the historic home of parliamentary democracy is a proud milestone for the entire Olive Tree family. This achievement reflects our continued dedication to educational excellence and our aspiration to make a meaningful difference in the lives of learners.

A proud milestone in our educational journey.
EDUCATIONAL LEADERSHIP

Education Icon of the Year

27th EDU Leaders Summit & Awards · 2025–2026

Olive Tree Global School, Chennai, is proud to share that Dr. Joy Leena Isaac, Correspondent of Olive Tree Global School, has been honoured with the prestigious Education Icon of the Year 2025–2026 award at the 27th EDU Leaders Summit & Awards. This recognition celebrates her leadership, dedication to educational excellence, and commitment to creating meaningful learning opportunities for students.

Celebrating visionary leadership and excellence in education.
INSTITUTIONAL ACHIEVEMENT

Best CBSE School

Asian Education Summit · Mumbai

Olive Tree Global School, Madhavaram, received the Best CBSE School Award at the Asian Education Summit in Mumbai, hosted by the Asia Education Conclave. The recognition celebrates the school's commitment to academic excellence, holistic development and innovation in education. The school acknowledges the visionary leadership of Chairman Mr. Isaac Livingstone P. and extends its gratitude to its students, teachers and parents. Their dedication, encouragement and shared commitment are an important part of the Olive Tree journey.

Excellence achieved through collective effort.
GLOBAL LEADERSHIP

Global Education Leadership Award

Honoured at the House of Commons, UK

Chairman Thiru. Isaac Livingstone was honoured at the House of Commons, British Parliament, United Kingdom, with the Excellence in Education Service Global Education Leadership Award. The award was presented by the World Tamil Organisation – UK, with Mrs. Dame Diana Griffiths DBE, Member of Parliament and Shadow Minister for Exports, United Kingdom, associated with the event. Olive Tree Group of Schools was among the institutions from Tamil Nadu recognised at the event, alongside VIT Group of Institutions and RMK Group of Institutions. This occasion stands as a significant milestone in the school's educational journey.

Leadership that inspires educational progress.
SCHOOL EXCELLENCE

Best School of the Year 2024

South Iconic Awards

Olive Tree Global School, Madhavaram, received the Best School of the Year Award from South Iconic Awards in 2024. The recognition honours the school's achievements and service in the field of education, as acknowledged by the awarding organisation. This milestone celebrates the collective contribution of the Olive Tree community. The school values the continued efforts of its students, educators, leadership team and parents in supporting a positive and enriching educational environment.

A shared achievement for the Olive Tree family.
LEADERSHIP & VISION

Visionary Leadership Award

World School Summit · Dubai

Dr. Isaac Livingstone, Chairman of Olive Tree, received the Visionary Leadership Award for his leadership in education at the 20th Edition of the World School Summit in Dubai. This recognition highlights the importance of a clear educational vision, purposeful leadership and a continued commitment to student development. The school celebrates this milestone as part of its ongoing journey towards educational progress and excellence.

A vision for learning. A commitment to the future.
INSTITUTIONAL RECOGNITION

Most Emerging Higher Education Institution

Asian Education Awards

Olive Tree Global School received the recognition for Most Emerging Higher Education Institution (India), according to the school's announcement. The event brought together participating schools from across Asia. The school expressed its gratitude to former Chief Justice of India Dr. K. G. Balakrishnan, the jury members of the Asian Education Awards and Major General Sanjay Soi. This recognition is dedicated to the students, teaching and non-teaching staff, parents and everyone who forms part of the Olive Tree family.

Celebrating the people behind every achievement.
STEM & COGNITIVE DEVELOPMENT

Excellence in Cognitive Development

Eldrok India K-12 Awards 2022

Olive Tree Global Education Campus, Chennai, received recognition for Excellence in Cognitive Development in connection with STEM education. STEM brings together Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics to encourage logical thinking, investigation and problem-solving. The recognition was presented on the occasion of the K-12 Education Summit organised by Eldrok India as part of the India K-12 Awards 2022 at ITC Grand Chola, Chennai. This achievement reflects the value of encouraging curiosity and developing analytical skills through meaningful learning experiences.

Inspiring curiosity through STEM learning.
