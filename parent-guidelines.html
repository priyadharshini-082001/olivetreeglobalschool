Parents' Guidelines

Parents play an important role in creating a positive learning environment. Your support, encouragement and cooperation help children grow with confidence, discipline and responsibility.

01
🏫

Regular Attendance

  • Ensure your child attends school regularly.
  • Encourage punctuality and timely arrival.
  • Inform the school when absence is unavoidable.
02
⏰

Be Punctual

  • Help your child follow the school timings.
  • Avoid unnecessary late arrivals.
  • Plan travel time in advance.
03
📚

Support Learning

  • Encourage daily reading and revision.
  • Provide a quiet place for homework.
  • Take an interest in your child's learning.
04
💬

Stay Connected

  • Maintain regular communication with teachers.
  • Attend parent-teacher meetings.
  • Read school notices and announcements carefully.
05
❤️

Encourage & Motivate

  • Appreciate your child's efforts and progress.
  • Encourage participation in school activities.
  • Avoid comparing your child with others.
06
👔

School Uniform

  • Ensure the prescribed uniform is worn properly.
  • Keep the uniform neat and clean.
  • Follow the school's grooming guidelines.
07
📱

Responsible Technology

  • Monitor your child's screen time.
  • Encourage safe and responsible internet use.
  • Guide children in using digital devices wisely.
08
🥗

Health & Well-being

  • Provide healthy food and adequate rest.
  • Encourage regular physical activity.
  • Inform the school about important health concerns.
09
🌱

Build Good Habits

  • Teach children respect, honesty and kindness.
  • Encourage responsibility for personal belongings.
  • Develop good manners at home and school.
10
🤝

Respect School Community

  • Respect teachers, staff and fellow parents.
  • Follow school policies and procedures.
  • Work together for the welfare of every child.
11
🎒

Check School Requirements

  • Check your child's school bag regularly.
  • Ensure required books and materials are carried.
  • Help children keep their belongings organized.
12
🌟

Work as a Team

  • Support the school's educational initiatives.
  • Share concerns through appropriate channels.
  • Help create a safe and happy school environment.
