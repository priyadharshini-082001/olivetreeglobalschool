STUDENT GUIDELINES

School Rules & Regulations

Our rules help students develop discipline, responsibility, respect and good character while creating a safe and positive learning environment.

Discipline & Good Conduct

Students are expected to maintain discipline and demonstrate good behaviour throughout the school campus. They should respect teachers, staff and fellow students.

  • Follow school rules and instructions.
  • Maintain respectful behaviour.
  • Use polite and appropriate language.
  • Maintain discipline inside and outside classrooms.
  • Bullying and inappropriate behaviour are not permitted.

Punctuality & Attendance

Regular attendance and punctuality are essential for academic success. Students should be present at school on time and attend all scheduled classes.

  • Arrive at school before the scheduled time.
  • Attend classes regularly.
  • Avoid unnecessary absence.
  • Obtain permission for leave when required.
  • Be punctual for examinations and school activities.

Academic Responsibility

Students should take responsibility for their learning and actively participate in all academic activities. Regular effort and sincere preparation are expected.

  • Complete homework and assignments on time.
  • Bring required books and learning materials.
  • Pay attention during classroom activities.
  • Prepare sincerely for examinations.
  • Maintain academic honesty.

Uniform & Personal Appearance

Students must maintain a neat, clean and presentable appearance and follow the school's prescribed uniform and grooming standards.

  • Wear the prescribed school uniform.
  • Keep the uniform clean and properly maintained.
  • Wear the required footwear and accessories.
  • Maintain neat personal grooming.
  • Follow the school's appearance guidelines.

Respect & Responsibility

Every student should treat others with kindness, courtesy and respect. Responsible behaviour helps create a friendly and inclusive school community.

  • Respect teachers and school staff.
  • Be kind and helpful to classmates.
  • Respect different opinions and backgrounds.
  • Do not engage in bullying or harassment.
  • Take responsibility for personal actions.

Mobile Phones & Electronic Devices

Electronic devices should be used responsibly and only according to school guidelines. Learning and classroom activities must remain the priority.

  • Follow the school's mobile phone policy.
  • Do not use phones during classes without permission.
  • Electronic devices must not disturb learning.
  • Do not record others without permission.
  • Use technology responsibly and safely.

Clean & Green Campus

Students share the responsibility of keeping the school clean, healthy and environmentally friendly.

  • Keep classrooms and corridors clean.
  • Use dustbins properly.
  • Avoid wasting water and electricity.
  • Protect plants and school gardens.
  • Help maintain a clean and green campus.

Care of School Property

School facilities and resources are provided for the benefit of every student. Students must handle all school property carefully.

  • Take care of desks, chairs and classroom equipment.
  • Handle library books carefully.
  • Use laboratory equipment responsibly.
  • Do not damage walls or school facilities.
  • Report damaged property to the concerned teacher.

Learn • Respect • Grow • Lead

Every student is expected to follow these guidelines and contribute towards a safe, disciplined and positive school community.

About Us

Olive Tree Global School provides quality education in a safe, modern and value-based learning environment. We nurture students with academic excellence, discipline, leadership and moral values to prepare them for a successful future.
