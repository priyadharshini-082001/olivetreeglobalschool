OUR APPROACH

Academic Philosophy

Learning that encourages curiosity, understanding and meaningful discovery.

01
STUDENT FIRST

Learning Begins With the Student

At Olive Tree, learning is designed around the student. Every child is encouraged to participate, discover and grow with confidence.

02
UNDERSTAND

Beyond Memorisation

Our academic philosophy moves beyond memorisation and encourages students to understand concepts and make meaningful connections.

03
EXPLORE & QUESTION

Curiosity Creates Discovery

Students are encouraged to ask questions, explore ideas and develop the confidence to think independently.

04
REAL-LIFE LEARNING

Knowledge In Action

Classroom learning is connected with real-life experiences, helping children understand how knowledge can be applied.

About Us

Olive Tree Global School provides quality education in a safe, modern and value-based learning environment. We nurture students with academic excellence, discipline, leadership and moral values to prepare them for a successful future.
